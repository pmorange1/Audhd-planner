# Globetrotter Passport

A single-file daily planner. Daily tasks earn kilometres around the world; hobbies earn passport stamps. Rest and hobbies are never locked behind chores.

## Set up (about 5 minutes)
1. Upload the whole folder to any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages), or open index.html directly.
2. On first launch, enter your names, evening touchdown time and route.
3. On a phone, use Add to Home Screen to install it. It works offline after the first load.

## Customise
- Tasks: Passport tab, Customise Tasks.
- Appearance: Passport tab (Airmail, Night flight, Paper map).
- Routes: World loop or Europe only. Stops are in the LEGS list in index.html.
- Hobbies, skill ranks and rewards: Settings, Customise. Starter packs (Creative, Fitness, Study, Home life, Blank) can be added at first launch or from Customise. They add to your current hobbies and never replace them.
- Blank start: choose "Blank, I will build my own" at setup, or Customise, Start blank. Packs can then be added on top.
- Daily intro: a closed passport appears on first open each day. Tap to open it, see your latest stamps and begin. Turn it off in Settings, Intro and landing.
- Immersion: arrival postcards (saved in the Stamp book), leg-complete maps, press-and-hold to stamp tasks, a safe-landing screen at touchdown, layover mode for hobby timers, a draggable globe, a sky that follows the clock, soft sounds. Settings, Motion: Full, Calm or Off.
- Layout: the passport icon (top bar) holds stamps and postcards; the cog opens Settings. A 3-step tour runs on first launch (Settings, Replay tour).
- Insights (Passport tab), projected arrival dates (Journey tab), badges, shareable passport image, photo postcards (stored on the device, not included in backups), transfer code between devices (Settings).
- Story: a mystery unfolds behind every stamp (clue, speaker portrait, Signal note, field assignment), with a theory and big reveal at the end of each leg. Casebook is on the Passport tab. Visa ranks, reveals and assignments add a small distance bonus.
- Passport tab: Stamps (with souvenir shelf), Story (companions, Signal Tower map, seasonal chapter, casebook), Visas, Badges (12 achievement families). Today shows a companion line, plus an occasional detour or chapter task.
- Casebook tab (Case, Clues, Crew): the story so far, seven puzzles (cipher, anagram, sequence, riddle, odd one out, missing letters, final lock), case board, companions, Signal Tower map. Passport tab: Stamps, Visas, Badges, Log (calendar).
- Focus timer: Start timer on any hobby.
- Export: Settings, Export history (CSV).

## Privacy
All data stays in your browser on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use Backup / Restore to export or import a JSON file.
